# Copyright and takedown

The 74 gold-medal papers in the year folders are not ours and are not covered by
this repository's MIT licence. They were written by the teams that won the
University Physics Competition and are published by the organisers at
<http://uphysicsc.com>. Copyright stays with each paper's authors.

The copies here are the organisers' files, unmodified, and no claim of ownership
is made over any of them. They are kept so the papers can be read offline and so
the statistics in `skill/upc-paper/references/` can be checked against the files
they were measured from. Every entry in [README.md](README.md) links to the
official copy as well.

If you are the author of a paper and want it removed, open an issue. It will be
removed from the folders, from the index, and from the release archive.

The code, the template, the agent skill, and the prose around those measurements
are ours and are MIT-licensed like the rest of the repository.
