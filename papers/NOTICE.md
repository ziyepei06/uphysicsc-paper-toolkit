# Notice

This repository does **not** redistribute the gold-medal papers.

The 74 papers linked from [`README.md`](README.md) were written by competing
teams and are published by the University Physics Competition at
<http://uphysicsc.com>. Copyright remains with their authors. The index here
lists each paper and links to the copy the organisers publish; no paper PDF is
stored in this repository, in its git history, or in its releases.

Why: a rights-holder takedown would take down the LaTeX template and the agent
skill along with the papers, and those are the part of this repository that is
actually ours to give away. Linking to the official file costs a reader one
click and cannot be taken down.

The statistics in `skill/upc-paper/references/` — page counts, figure counts,
section conventions, summary lengths — are measurements taken from those papers
and are facts about them, not copies of them. The prose around those numbers is
ours and is MIT-licensed like the rest of the repository.

If you are the author of a listed paper and want the title or the link removed,
open an issue and it will be removed.
