## Reading the table

- **Pages**: 16–30 is the main cluster (50 of 74). The extremes (5 pages, 111 pages) are outliers, not models to copy.
- **Figs**: median 14; 51 papers have ≥10 and 19 have ≥20. Under 8 figures usually means missing error analysis.
- **Tabs**: 1–4 is typical; big tables go to the appendix.
- **Refs**: 8–12 is typical; fewer than 5 looks under-researched, more than 25 means you are writing a review.
- **Not./Assum./S&W/Valid./Sens./App.**: presence markers. Validation appears in 18 papers and sensitivity in 10 — adding them is a cheap way to stand out.
